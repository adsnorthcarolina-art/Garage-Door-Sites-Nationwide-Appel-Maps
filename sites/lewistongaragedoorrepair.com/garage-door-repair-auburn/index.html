What we fix most in Auburn

Opener won't respond

Remote dead, wall button does nothing, or the motor hums but the door doesn't move. We repair logic boards, gears and sensors, and tell you plainly when a new opener is the better value.

Broken torsion springs

The loud bang from the garage followed by a door that's suddenly too heavy to lift. We replace springs in matched pairs, sized to the door's actual weight, so the door balances and the opener stops straining.

Door reverses before closing

Nine times out of ten the safety sensors are misaligned or dirty. The rest of the time it's a travel-limit or force setting. Quick to diagnose, usually quick to fix.

Old door replacement

When a door is 20+ years old, uninsulated, or several panels are damaged, replacement is the better investment. We measure, order and install, and haul the old door away.

Garage doors and freezing winters

Cold makes steel springs brittle and ice can freeze the bottom seal to the slab. Most winter calls here are snapped springs and doors that won't lift; we carry cold-rated springs and seals.

About homes in Auburn

A lot of Auburn garages are attached and share a wall with living space. An insulated door and a quiet belt-drive opener make a real difference in noise and energy bills.
