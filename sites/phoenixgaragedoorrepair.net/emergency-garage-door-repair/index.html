24-hour emergency garage door repair in Phoenix

Door stuck open with the house exposed, car trapped inside before work, or a door that came down hard and won't move — we answer the phone around the clock and dispatch the nearest Phoenix technician.

Emergency line, 24/7602-848-6450

What counts as an emergency

  • Door stuck open and can't be closed — your garage and often your house are unsecured
  • Door stuck closed with a car you need inside
  • Door off its track or hanging crooked
  • Spring or cable snapped and the door dropped
  • Door hit by a vehicle
  • Opener running on its own or won't stop

What to do while you wait

  • Don't press the opener button again — it can make a jammed door worse
  • Don't try to lift a door with a broken spring; it weighs 150–400 lb without the spring's help
  • Keep children, pets and cars away from the opening
  • If the door is stuck open, lock the door between garage and house and move valuables out of sight

What the emergency visit includes

Same price structure as a daytime call: the technician inspects, quotes the whole repair before starting, and carries springs, cables, rollers and opener parts to finish on the spot. If a part has to be ordered, we secure the door so it's closed and safe until the follow-up visit.
