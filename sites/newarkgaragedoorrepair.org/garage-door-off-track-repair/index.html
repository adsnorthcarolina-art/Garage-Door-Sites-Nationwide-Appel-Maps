Garage door off track repair in Newark

A door that's jumped its track is crooked, jammed, and one bump away from coming down. Don't run the opener and don't try to lift it. We put it back on the rails and fix whatever knocked it off.

Why doors come off track

  • A snapped or frayed lift cable, which lets one side drop
  • Worn or broken rollers that pop out of the track
  • Track bent by a car bumper, a mower or a stored ladder
  • Loose track brackets or lag bolts after years of vibration
  • A broken spring that lets the door slam and jump
  • Running the opener with something blocking the door

What we do

The technician secures the door first so it can't fall, then releases the tension, re-seats the rollers, straightens or replaces bent track sections and re-anchors the brackets. If a cable, roller or spring caused it, that part is replaced in the same visit — otherwise it will happen again within weeks. We finish by running the door through full cycles and setting the opener's limits.

Is it safe to wait?

Keep people and cars clear of the door and don't press the opener button. A door off one track is being held by the other side's hardware and can drop without warning. This is one of the calls we dispatch first in Newark.
