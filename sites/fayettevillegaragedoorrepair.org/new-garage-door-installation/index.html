Door styles we install

  • Traditional raised-panel steel — the most popular door in Fayetteville, available in single- and double-car widths, non-insulated to triple-layer
  • Insulated steel — polyurethane or polystyrene cores for garages that share a wall with living space; quieter and far better in summer and winter
  • Carriage house — swing-out looks with standard overhead operation, in steel, composite or wood
  • Modern and full-view — flush panels or aluminum frames with glass
  • Commercial sectional and rolling steel — for shops, warehouses and multi-bay buildings

Brands

We're set up to order and install Clopay, Amarr, Wayne Dalton, C.H.I., Raynor and Overhead Door, so you can pick on looks, insulation and budget rather than on what one brand happens to offer.

What installation includes

  • In-home measurement and a written quote, no obligation
  • Removal and disposal of the old door and hardware
  • New track, springs sized to the door, rollers, hinges and weather seal
  • Opener reconnection and force/limit setup, or a new opener if you want one
  • Manufacturer's warranty on the door plus our 1-year labor warranty

Repair or replace?

If the panels are sound and the problem is a spring, cable or opener, repair is almost always the better value, and we'll say so. Replacement makes sense when several panels are damaged, the door is uninsulated and the garage is attached, or the hardware is worn past the point where one repair fixes it.
