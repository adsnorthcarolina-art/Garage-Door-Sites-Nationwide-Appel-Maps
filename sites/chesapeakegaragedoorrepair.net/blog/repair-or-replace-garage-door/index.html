Repair or Replace Your Garage Door? An Honest Guide

When a garage door repair is the right call and when it's throwing money at an old door. A practical guide for Chesapeake homeowners, from the people who do both.

November 4, 2026 · Chesapeake Garage Door Repair

We do both repairs and new doors in Chesapeake, so we have no reason to push you either way. Here is how we actually decide.

Repair, almost always, when:

  • It's a spring, cable, roller or hinge. These are wear parts. Replacing them restores the door; it says nothing about the door's remaining life.
  • The opener failed. The opener and the door are separate systems. A new opener on a sound door is a great combination.
  • One panel is dented. If the door is a current model, a single matching section is far cheaper than a new door.
  • The door is under 15 years old. A steel door with intact panels has decades left in it.

Replace when:

  • Several panels are bent, rusted or cracked. Once two or three sections need replacing, you're most of the way to a new door's cost, on an old door.
  • The door is 20+ years old and uninsulated and the garage is attached or under a bedroom. A modern insulated door changes the temperature of the rooms around it and cuts noise.
  • It's a wood door that's rotting at the bottom. Rot spreads and the door gets heavier every year, which kills springs and openers.
  • Panels or parts are no longer made. Some older models can't be matched.
  • You're selling. A new garage door consistently ranks among the highest-return exterior projects; it's often the single largest element of the front of the house.

The grey zone

A 15-to-20-year-old door with a broken spring and tired rollers is the common grey case. Our rule: if the panels are straight and not rusting, repair it and expect several more years. If you're going to be looking at the door every day and already dislike it, that's a real factor too.

Either way, the technician lays out both options with real prices on site. Repairs come with a 5-year parts warranty; new doors carry the manufacturer's warranty plus ours. Read about new door installation in Chesapeake, or call and we'll come take a look.
