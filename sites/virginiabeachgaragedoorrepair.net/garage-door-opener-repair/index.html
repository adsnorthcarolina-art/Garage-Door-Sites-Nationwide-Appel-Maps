Common opener problems we fix

  • Door reverses before closing — usually misaligned or dirty safety sensors, sometimes the close-force setting
  • Motor runs but door doesn't move — stripped drive gear, broken trolley or a snapped belt/chain
  • Remote or keypad doesn't work — batteries, reprogramming, or a failed logic board
  • Opener hums or clicks — capacitor, motor or a door that's too heavy because of a broken spring
  • Opens on its own — wall button short, stuck remote or a board fault
  • Very loud operation — worn chain, loose hardware or a motor on its way out

Repair or replace?

If your opener is under 10 years old and the problem is a gear, sensor, remote or board, repair is the better value and we carry those parts. If it's older than that, lacks safety sensors, or the motor itself has failed, a new opener usually costs little more than the repair and comes with a quieter drive, battery backup and smartphone control. The technician will lay out both options with prices before doing anything.

New opener installation

We install and service every major brand sold in Virginia Beach, including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman and Linear. Belt-drive openers for garages under bedrooms, chain-drive for value, and wall-mount (jackshaft) openers for high ceilings or storage racks. Installation includes removing the old unit, setting travel limits and force, aligning the safety sensors, and programming your remotes, keypad and car.
