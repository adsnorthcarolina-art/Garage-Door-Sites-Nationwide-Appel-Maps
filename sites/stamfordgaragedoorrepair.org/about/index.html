About Stamford Garage Door Repair

Garage doors, and only garage doors, in Stamford since 2004.

What we do

Stamford Garage Door Repair repairs, services and installs residential and commercial garage doors and openers across Stamford, CT and the surrounding area. Springs, cables, rollers, track, panels, openers and new doors — if it's on or above a garage door, it's what we do all day.

How we work

  • Same-day appointments. Most Stamford calls are seen the day you call, evenings and weekends included. Phones are answered 24 hours a day.
  • Firm price first. The technician inspects the whole system and quotes the complete job before any work starts. The service call fee is applied to the repair.
  • One visit. Trucks carry the common springs, cables, rollers, opener parts and remotes so most repairs are finished on the spot.
  • Warranty. 5 years on springs and parts we install, 1 year on labor, plus the manufacturer's warranty on new doors and openers.
  • Insured technicians trained on every major brand: Clopay, Amarr, Wayne Dalton, LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman and more.
  • Free 25-point safety inspection with every repair.

Discounts and financing

Senior (60+) and military discounts on any service — mention it when you book. Financing is available on new doors and larger repairs. We accept cash and all major credit cards.

Where we are

Our Stamford office is at 500 Summer St, Suite 203, Stamford, CT 06901. We serve Stamford and Greenwich, Norwalk, Darien, New Canaan — see the full service area.
