Common questions

How fast can you get to me in Charlotte?

Most calls in Charlotte are handled the same day. Tell us what the door is doing when you call and we'll give you an arrival window right away.

How much does garage door repair cost in Charlotte?

It depends on the part. Spring, cable and roller repairs are the most common and the most affordable. You get a firm price before any work starts, and the service call fee is applied to the repair.

Do you work on all garage door brands?

Yes. We service every major door and opener brand, including Clopay, Amarr, Wayne Dalton, LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie and Craftsman.

My door is stuck halfway. Is it safe to force it?

No. A door stuck part-way usually means a broken spring or cable, and the door can drop. Leave it where it is, keep people and cars clear, and call us.

Should I repair or replace my garage door?

If the panels are sound and it's a spring, cable or opener problem, repair is almost always the better value. If several panels are damaged or the door is 20+ years old and uninsulated, we'll walk you through replacement options and prices on site.
