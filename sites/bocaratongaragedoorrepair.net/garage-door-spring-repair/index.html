Garage door spring repair in Boca Raton

A loud bang from the garage, then a door that's suddenly far too heavy or only lifts a few inches — that's a broken spring. We replace torsion and extension springs across Boca Raton the same day, with the right size on the truck.

Signs your spring has broken

  • A loud bang or snap from the garage, often with nobody near the door
  • The door opens 6 inches and stops, or the opener strains and gives up
  • A visible gap in the coil of the spring above the door
  • The door feels extremely heavy to lift by hand
  • Cables hanging loose or the door sitting crooked in the opening
  • The door slams shut instead of closing smoothly

Torsion and extension springs

Most Boca Raton homes built in the last 30 years have one or two torsion springs on a shaft above the door. Older and lighter doors use a pair of extension springs that run along the horizontal tracks. Both are under enormous tension and both have a rated life of roughly 10,000 cycles — about 7 to 10 years for a typical household. When one spring on a two-spring door breaks, the other is the same age and will follow soon, which is why we replace them in pairs.

What the repair includes

  • Springs sized to the actual weight of your door, not a one-size part
  • Both springs replaced on two-spring doors, with the old ones hauled away
  • Cables, drums, bearings and the center bracket checked while the system is apart
  • Door balanced and the opener's force settings adjusted afterward
  • Our free 25-point safety inspection
  • 5-year warranty on the springs, 1 year on labor

Why not do it yourself?

Spring replacement is the one garage door job we tell Boca Raton homeowners not to attempt. A torsion spring stores enough energy to break a hand or send a winding bar across the garage. Our technicians carry the right winding bars, the right spring charts and the insurance to go with them.
