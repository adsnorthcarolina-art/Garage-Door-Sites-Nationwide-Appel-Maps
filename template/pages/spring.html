Signs your spring has broken

  • A loud bang or snap from the garage, often with nobody near the door
  • The door opens 6 inches and stops, or the opener strains and gives up
  • A visible gap in the coil of the spring above the door
  • The door feels extremely heavy to lift by hand
  • Cables hanging loose or the door sitting crooked in the opening
  • The door slams shut instead of closing smoothly

Torsion and extension springs

Most {{city}} homes built in the last 30 years have one or two torsion springs on a shaft above the door. Older and lighter doors use a pair of extension springs that run along the horizontal tracks. Both are under enormous tension and both have a rated life of roughly 10,000 cycles — about 7 to 10 years for a typical household. When one spring on a two-spring door breaks, the other is the same age and will follow soon, which is why we replace them in pairs.
